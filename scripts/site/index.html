Face Maker

Cut-out collage faces from public-domain and open-licensed portraits, in the spirit of a Monty Python animation.

Collage maker

Pick, move, resize and swap face parts. Randomize, and export batches as transparent PNGs.

PS1 head

Put a collage face on a low-poly, PlayStation-style 3D head, with hair and hats.
Every part comes from a work that is public domain or openly licensed; each app has a credits list. Source and licence: github.com/AHeathNE/face-maker.
